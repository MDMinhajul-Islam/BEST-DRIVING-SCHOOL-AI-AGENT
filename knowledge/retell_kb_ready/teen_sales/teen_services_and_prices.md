## Teen packages

**24 Hr Classroom + Driving Package — $399 base price — Best selling.** Includes 24 classroom hours, 7 instructor driving hours and 7 observation hours. The school states a Texas learner permit is needed before behind-the-wheel hours begin, not before classroom enrollment. Source: [Teen Driver Education Package](https://bestdrivingschool.us/driving-courses/teen-24-hour-class-room-and-driving-package).

**Behind the Wheel Only (7 & 7) — $350 base price.** Includes 7 instructor driving hours, 7 observation hours and use of a dual-control vehicle. School prerequisites say a Texas learner permit and completed classroom education are required. Source: [Teen Behind-the-Wheel Lessons](https://bestdrivingschool.us/driving-courses/teen-behind-the-wheel-only).

INTERNAL BUSINESS VERIFIED: each package's 7 driving hours is scheduled as 2 + 2 + 2 + 1 hours. This does not schedule or describe the 7 observation hours.
