# Impact Texas Drivers

Regulatory research date: 2026-09-13

General Class C guidance. Use only records marked `official_verified` for general answers; flagged details require confirmation. Source dates are not assumed effective dates.

## TX-R030: ITD is separate from education

Applies when: `{"skills_test_required": true}`

- Complete the appropriate ITD program after required education/in-car instruction and before skills testing. It does not replace driver education.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-DPS-005](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/impact-texas-drivers-itd-program), [TX-DPS-015](https://impacttexasdrivers.dps.texas.gov/itad/FAQ.aspx)

## TX-R031: Usual minor ITTD and adult ITAD

Applies when: `{"skills_test_required": true}`

- Minor teen/PTDE applicants use ITTD (two hours). Adult six-hour-course/ADE-1317 applicants use ITAD (one hour); DPS overview also identifies ITAD for ages 25+.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-DPS-005](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/impact-texas-drivers-itd-program), [TX-DPS-015](https://impacttexasdrivers.dps.texas.gov/itad/FAQ.aspx), [TX-DPS-016](https://impacttexasdrivers.dps.texas.gov/ittd/FAQ.aspx)

## TX-R032: ITD validity and paper copy

Applies when: `{"skills_test_required": true}`

- Present a printed ITD certificate dated within 90 days of the skills test. If the test is not passed within that window, restart the program. ITAD and ITTD are free.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-DPS-015](https://impacttexasdrivers.dps.texas.gov/itad/FAQ.aspx), [TX-DPS-016](https://impacttexasdrivers.dps.texas.gov/ittd/FAQ.aspx)

## TX-R033: Adult ITD selection ambiguity

Applies when: `{"age_min": 18, "teen_certificate_or_program_choice_unclear": true}`

- Adult FAQ permits either ITAD or ITTD at 18+, while the overview and certificate-based FAQs specify programs differently. Confirm the acceptable program with DPS/testing provider for adults holding teen certificates; do not invent a universal choice.

Confidence: `conflicting_official_sources`. Review required: True.

Sources: [TX-DPS-015](https://impacttexasdrivers.dps.texas.gov/itad/FAQ.aspx), [TX-DPS-016](https://impacttexasdrivers.dps.texas.gov/ittd/FAQ.aspx), [TX-DPS-005](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/impact-texas-drivers-itd-program)
