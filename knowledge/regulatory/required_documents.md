# Required Documents

Regulatory research date: 2026-09-13

General Class C guidance. Use only records marked `official_verified` for general answers; flagged details require confirmation. Source dates are not assumed effective dates.

## TX-R012: Teen school attendance evidence

Applies when: `{"age_max": 17, "learner_application": true}`

- Bring diploma/GED or Verification of Enrollment and Attendance. VOE is valid for 30 days, or 90 days when issued June through August.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-DPS-002](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/texas-learners-license-teen)

## TX-R013: Teen guardian and education evidence

Applies when: `{"age_max": 17, "learner_application": true}`

- Bring the applicable DE-964/DEE-964/DE-964E education evidence and required identity, residency, lawful-status and SSN documentation. Parent/guardian presence or an accepted waiver/notarized application is required as specified by DPS.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-DPS-002](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/texas-learners-license-teen)

## TX-R034: Core application document categories

Applies when: `{"texas_license_application": true}`

- Provide identity, Texas residency, citizenship/lawful-presence evidence and SSN as applicable, plus the application. Owned-vehicle insurance or a no-vehicle statement is specified; vehicle registration evidence at application is limited to new residents exchanging out-of-state licenses on the general DPS page.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-DPS-001](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/apply-texas-driver-license)

## TX-R035: Identity combinations

Applies when: `{"texas_license_application": true}`

- Use one primary identity document, two secondary, or one secondary plus two supporting. Present originals/certified copies and legal name-change evidence when needed; photocopies are not accepted.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-DPS-009](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/identification-requirements)

## TX-R036: Texas residency documents

Applies when: `{"texas_license_application": true}`

- Normally present two printed documents with the applicant's name and Texas residential address; one must show at least 30 days' residency. Exchange of a valid unexpired out-of-state license/ID waives the 30-day duration, not the two-document requirement.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-DPS-010](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/texas-residency-requirement-driver-licenses-and-id-cards)

## TX-R038: SSN verification

Applies when: `{"ssn_eligible": true}`

- DPS electronically verifies SSN information; resolve mismatches with SSA before issuance.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-DPS-011](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/social-security-number-ssn)

## TX-R040: Lawful presence verification

Applies when: `{"not_us_citizen": true}`

- Present appropriate lawful-status evidence for the applicant category. DHS verification must complete before DPS issues the license; no approval or immigration outcome is guaranteed.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-DPS-012](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/us-citizenship-or-lawful-presence-requirement)

## Conditional checklist

All applicants: identity, residency, citizenship/lawful status, SSN as applicable and application (R034–R040). Teen learner: education milestone, guardian/waiver and diploma/GED/VOE (R012–R013). Teen provisional: completed education, learner and ITD evidence (R016/R044). Skills test: identification, valid ITD and vehicle documents as applicable (R032/R041–R043). Exchange applicants: existing license, surrender choice and owned-vehicle registration (R034/R047). Confirm original document acceptance with DPS; do not accept caller claims as verified documents.
