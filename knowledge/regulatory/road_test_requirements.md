# Road Test Requirements

Regulatory research date: 2026-09-13

General Class C guidance. Use only records marked `official_verified` for general answers; flagged details require confirmation. Source dates are not assumed effective dates.

## TX-R016: Usual teen road-test prerequisites

Applies when: `{"age_min": 16, "age_max": 17, "hardship_exception": false}`

- Complete classroom and in-car education, the applicable learner holding period and ITTD within 90 days, then pass the skills test; DPS decides provisional issuance.

Sources: [TX-TDLR-007](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/driver/education/parent-taught/provisional-license.htm), [TX-DPS-004](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/graduated-driver-license-gdl-and-hardship-license)

## TX-R041: DPS test vehicle safety

Applies when: `{"dps_skills_test": true}`

- Bring a legally operable vehicle that passes the examiner's safety check: working signals, horn, speedometer, mirrors, doors, seatbelts and brake lights, plus weather-appropriate lights/wipers. Bring current registration and insurance; the applicant must not be an excluded driver.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-DPS-P01](https://www.dps.texas.gov/internetforms/Forms/DL-60.pdf)

## TX-R042: Test-day identification and failure triggers

Applies when: `{"dps_skills_test": true}`

- Present valid identification. Unsafe vehicles may prevent testing; pets/unauthorized passengers are excluded, and dangerous or illegal maneuvers cause an automatic failure.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-DPS-P01](https://www.dps.texas.gov/internetforms/Forms/DL-60.pdf)

## TX-R043: Annual inspection versus examiner safety check

Applies when: `{"noncommercial_test_vehicle": true}`

- DPS says annual noncommercial safety inspection ended January 1, 2025, while March 2026 DL-60 still lists unexpired inspection for testing. The examiner safety check remains separate; confirm exact test-vehicle paperwork with DPS/provider and do not promise a universal annual-inspection requirement.

Confidence: `conflicting_official_sources`. Review required: True.

Sources: [TX-DPS-P01](https://www.dps.texas.gov/internetforms/Forms/DL-60.pdf), [TX-DPS-017](https://www.dps.texas.gov/news/vehicle-safety-inspection-program-changes-now-effect)
