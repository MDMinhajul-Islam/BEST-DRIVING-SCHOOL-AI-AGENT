# Parent Taught Driver Education

Regulatory research date: 2026-09-13

General Class C guidance. Use only records marked `official_verified` for general answers; flagged details require confirmation. Source dates are not assumed effective dates.

## TX-R019: PTDE authorization and guide

Applies when: `{"parent_taught": true}`

- Obtain TDLR instructor designation for the particular student before teaching; read the supplied program guide and select an approved parent-taught course.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Applies when: `{"parent_taught": true}`

- Current public guidance states $20 nonrefundable per instructor/student designation application, online only. This is separate from course costs.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

## TX-R021: Instructor relationships

Applies when: `{"parent_taught": true}`

- Eligible relationships include parent, stepparent, grandparent, step-grandparent, foster parent and legal guardian. An authorized alternate must meet the same qualifications and may not charge a fee.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-TDLR-010](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/driver/education/parent-taught/instructor-eligibility/), [TX-TDLR-003](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/driver/education/parent-taught/parent-instructors.htm)

## TX-R022: Instructor screening is not approval

Applies when: `{"parent_taught": true}`

- The official eligibility questionnaire asks age 25+, seven years holding a license, three continuous valid-license years, homicide/DWI history, suspensions, accidents and moving violations. These are screening questions; obtain TDLR/DPS confirmation of the actual instructor's eligibility.

Confidence: `requires_human_review`. Review required: True.

Sources: [TX-TDLR-010](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/driver/education/parent-taught/instructor-eligibility/)

## TX-R023: DPS record checks and B restriction

Applies when: `{"parent_taught": true}`

- B-restricted license holders cannot instruct. DPS checks the instructor's record at learner and provisional stages; disqualification may invalidate subsequent instruction hours.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-TDLR-003](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/driver/education/parent-taught/parent-instructors.htm)

## TX-R024: No driving instruction before learner issuance

Applies when: `{"parent_taught": true}`

- Obtain the learner license before starting behind-the-wheel training; finish remaining classroom and in-car requirements afterward.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-TDLR-P01](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/driver/docs/parent-taught-step-by-step-guide.pdf), [TX-TDLR-006](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/driver/education/parent-taught/behind-the-wheel.htm)

## TX-R025: PTDE daily instruction limits conflict

Applies when: `{"parent_taught": true}`

- TDLR classroom guidance permits six daily instruction hours, while the in-car page caps combined classroom/in-car credit at two. Do not calculate completion dates until TDLR resolves the discrepancy.

Confidence: `conflicting_official_sources`. Review required: True.

Sources: [TX-TDLR-004](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/driver/education/parent-taught/classroom-instruction.htm), [TX-TDLR-006](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/driver/education/parent-taught/behind-the-wheel.htm)

## TX-R026: Hybrid transfer needs course review

Applies when: `{"parent_taught": true, "classroom_elsewhere": true}`

- TDLR describes transfer of licensed-school classroom education into parent-taught in-car instruction after designation. The page's 32-hour classroom wording conflicts with 24-hour overviews; confirm course acceptance and transfer certificate.

Confidence: `conflicting_official_sources`. Review required: True.
