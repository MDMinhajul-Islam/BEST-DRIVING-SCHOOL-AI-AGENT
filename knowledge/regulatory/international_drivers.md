# International Drivers

Regulatory research date: 2026-09-13

General Class C guidance. Use only records marked `official_verified` for general answers; flagged details require confirmation. Source dates are not assumed effective dates.

## TX-R050: Foreign reciprocal license categories

Applies when: `{"foreign_license": true, "new_texas_resident": true}`

- DPS lists France, Germany, South Korea, UAE and Taiwan for valid-license testing reciprocity; retaining the foreign license affects waivers. Confirm the applicant's age, country, license validity and surrender choice with DPS.

Confidence: `requires_dps_confirmation`. Review required: True.

Sources: [TX-DPS-007](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/moving-texas-guide-driver-licenses-and-ids)

## TX-R051: Foreign document translations

Applies when: `{"foreign_license": true}`

- DPS requires foreign licenses not in English or Spanish to be translated by a translation service or consulate; foreign marriage documents not in English need certified English translation.

Confidence: `official_verified`. Review required: False.

Sources: [TX-DPS-007](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/moving-texas-guide-driver-licenses-and-ids)

## TX-R052: Visitors versus residents

Applies when: `{"foreign_license": true, "texas_residency_status": "unknown_or_nonresident"}`

- Recognition of a foreign license depends on agreements and nonresident conditions; DPS describes a limited visitor period and a different 90-day resident rule. Ask residency and country; confirm individual recognition and IDP questions with DPS.

Confidence: `requires_dps_confirmation`. Review required: True.

Sources: [TX-DPS-008](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license/driving-privilege-reciprocity)
