## What Makes a Good Driving School in Texas?

Three Clear Standards A good driving school in Texas meets three clear standards: it holds active TDLR certification, its instructors are individually licensed (not just the school itself), and it teaches students to the actual DPS road test criteria — not just to pass a test, but to drive safely once the license is in their wallet. The TDLR (Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation) is the state agency that licenses and oversees all Texas driving schools. A school without TDLR certification cannot legally provide driver education in Texas or issue the DE-964 certificate that teen drivers need to apply for a license.
