# Licensing qualification questions

Manual design reference. Ask only unanswered questions that change this caller’s pathway. Caller statements are self-reported, not verified government records.

## `age`

Question: “How old is the person getting the license?”

Why needed: Age changes education and license stage.

Source basis: TX-R001/TX-R002/TX-R005, with official references in the structured rule records.

## `student_or_parent`

Question: “Are you calling for yourself or for your child?”

Why needed: Identify the student without mistaking caller age for student age.

Source basis: TX-R013/TX-R019, with official references in the structured rule records.

## `first_license/current_license_type`

Question: “Is this your first license, or do you already have a license or learner license?”

Why needed: Separate first-time, learner and transfer paths.

Source basis: TX-R003/TX-R010/TX-R047, with official references in the structured rule records.

## `texas_residency_status`

Question: “Do you live in Texas now, and did you recently move here?”

Why needed: Separate resident exchange from visitor recognition.

Source basis: TX-R046/TX-R052, with official references in the structured rule records.

## `license_issuing_jurisdiction/license_expiry`

Question: “Where was your current license issued, and is it still valid?”

Why needed: Ask only for transfer/foreign callers; expiry and jurisdiction affect waivers.

Source basis: TX-R047–TX-R052, with official references in the structured rule records.

## `driver_education_status`

Question: “Have you completed driver education, and which certificate do you have?”

Why needed: Distinguish partial/full adult or teen completion.

Source basis: TX-R001/TX-R013/TX-R028, with official references in the structured rule records.

## `learner_license_status/issue_date`

Question: “Do you have a Texas learner license, and when was it issued?”

Why needed: Ask dates when teen holding period matters; account for suspensions.

Source basis: TX-R010/TX-R055, with official references in the structured rule records.

## `parent_taught_status`

Question: “Are you using parent-taught education with a designated instructor?”

Why needed: Ask only for PTDE interest/status; authorization is separate from a practice package.

Source basis: TX-R019–TX-R024, with official references in the structured rule records.

## `road_test_goal`

Question: “Are you looking for education, driving practice or the driving test?”

Why needed: Route to the relevant component.

Source basis: TX-R004/TX-R014, with official references in the structured rule records.

## `impact_program/impact_certificate_date`

Question: “Have you completed Impact Texas Drivers, and when was the certificate issued?”

Why needed: Ask when skills testing is the goal; determine program and 90-day currency.

Source basis: TX-R030–TX-R033, with official references in the structured rule records.

At the testing stage, ask whether teen education, observation/practice logs, required hold, ITD and documents are complete. Do not ask for SSNs or immigration-document numbers in qualification conversation. Refer uncertain document categories to DPS.
