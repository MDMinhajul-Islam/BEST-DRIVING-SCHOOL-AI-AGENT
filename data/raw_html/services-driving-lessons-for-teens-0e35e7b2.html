Guidance parents can trust

Teen Driver Training Focused on Safety

Handing over the keys for the first time is a big moment. Our instructors prioritize safety, patience, and responsible habits — building the confidence and judgment teens need every time they get behind the wheel.

Texas driver education providers and instructors are regulated by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR). Licensing, learner permits, and road testing are administered by the Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS).

  1. Consistent observation & mirror use
  2. Appropriate following distance
  3. Controlled speed & braking
  4. Awareness at intersections
  5. Safe lane changes
  6. Calm, distraction-free decisions
