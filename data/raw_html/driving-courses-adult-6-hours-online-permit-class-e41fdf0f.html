Course description

About this program

The Adult 6-Hour Permit Class in Texas is a mandatory course for adults who fall under the age limit for a learner’s license and wish to earn their safety certificate. This class educates participants on how to safely and responsibly operate a motor vehicle, including an understanding of basic laws of the road, defensive driving strategies, and proper signage recognition. The content of the six-hour module includes topics such as drug-free driving, distracted driving awareness, and rules of the road. Participants are also provided with helpful resources such as manuals, study guides, and practice tests to aid in their learning experience. Upon successful completion of all requirements, learners will receive an official Texas driver’s license or permit from the Texas Department of Public Safety, granting them the privilege of driving on the roads of Texas.

What's included

  • Six hours of TDLR-approved adult driver education
  • Taken online, at your own pace
  • ADE-1317 certificate on completion

What you need

Applicants aged 18 and over applying for a first Texas driver license.
