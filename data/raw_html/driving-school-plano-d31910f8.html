Parent-Taught Driving in Plano

Professional driving practice, skills evaluations, log-driving support and Texas road-test preparation for Parent-Taught Driver Education students in Plano. Different parent-taught and log-driving options are available.

10 / 15 / 30 hours

Parent-Taught Driver Education, or PTDE, allows an eligible parent or another qualified instructor to guide a teen through an approved parent-taught program. Professional lessons can help when a parent-taught student needs more work with lane changes, parking and backing, speed control, busy intersections, multilane traffic, highway driving, right-of-way, night-driving confidence, or road-test readiness.

Permit holders · Teens & adults 04

Third-Party Road Test in Plano, TX

Official Class C driving skills tests in Plano for eligible teen and adult applicants, plus road-test preparation to review parking, observation, right-of-way, lane position, signaling and speed control before testing.

On-site · DPS-authorizedPrep lesson available

The Third-Party Skills Testing program allows a DPS-authorized driver education school to administer the Class C driving skills test. DPS remains responsible for issuing the Texas driver license.

Common documents may include a valid Texas learner or restricted license, a DE-964 or ADE-1317 course-completion certificate, the applicable Impact Texas Drivers certificate, teen supervised-driving documentation, Verification of Enrollment when applicable, identification and other required supporting documents, and vehicle and insurance documents when using a personal vehicle.

The Impact Texas certificate must be completed within the required period before the road test. Review the current checklist before scheduling to avoid arriving with missing or expired documents.

Texas licensing, step by step

Texas Licensing Process for Plano Residents

The exact licensing path depends on the applicant’s age, previous license history, and type of driver education. The following provides a general sequence. Requirements can change; confirm current rules with the Texas Department of Public Safety and TDLR.

Your route
Select the Correct Driver Education Method Teen or adult course
Obtain the Appropriate Learner License Your permit from DPS
Complete Education and Behind-the-Wheel Training Instructor-led hours
Receive the Applicable Course Certificate DE-964 · ADE-1317
Complete Impact Texas Drivers ITTD or ITAD
Pass the Driving Skills Test DPS or third-party
01

Select the Correct Driver Education Method

Teen applicants may use a licensed driving school, an eligible Parent-Taught Driver Education program, or another approved method.

Adults ages 18 through 24 applying for a first Texas license generally need the six-hour adult course.

02

Obtain the Appropriate Learner License

Teen students may apply for a learner license after reaching the required age and completing the applicable initial classroom requirement.

Adult beginners need the appropriate Texas learner or restricted license before practicing on public roads.

03

Complete Education and Behind-the-Wheel Training

Teen students complete the required classroom, instructor-led driving, observation, and supervised-practice components.

Adults can schedule private driving lessons according to their current skill level.

Adult Driving Lessons →
04

Receive the Applicable Course Certificate

Qualifying teen driver education programs issue a DE-964 certificate. Qualifying six-hour adult driver education programs issue an ADE-1317 certificate.

Keep the applicable document available for the DPS or third-party testing process.

05

Complete Impact Texas Drivers

Teen applicants generally complete Impact Texas Teen Drivers, or ITTD. Adult applicants complete the Impact Texas program required for their age and driver education path.

The certificate must remain valid on the test date.

06

Pass the Driving Skills Test

Eligible applicants may complete the test through DPS or an authorized Third-Party Skills Testing provider.

Passing the test is not the same as receiving the physical license; applicants must complete the remaining DPS issuance process.
