The official route

## How an Adult Gets a Texas Driver License

Adults do not go through the learner and provisional stages that under-18s do. There is no six-month holding period and no supervised-hours log — the route is short. Here is what it takes, from the course to the driving test.

Ages 18 and over

### Your route to a first Texas license

Five steps, in order — from the course you take first to the day you test.

01

### Adult driver education

Complete the 6-hour adult driver education course and receive your ADE-1317 certificate.

02

### Impact Texas Adult Drivers

Watch the Impact Texas Adult Drivers (ITAD) video — the certificate must be dated within 90 days of your driving test.

03

### Bring your documents

Bring proof of identity, lawful presence, Texas residency and your Social Security number.

04

### Vision and knowledge tests

Pass the vision exam and the written knowledge test.

05

### Driving skills test

Pass the driving skills test — you can take it here in Plano.

New Texas residents who surrender a valid, unexpired license from another state do not need the adult course. Lessons are optional, but they are usually the difference between passing the skills test and repeating it.

Take the driving test here, not at DPS

Best Driving School is a DPS-authorized third party testing provider, so you can take the official skills test with us in Plano rather than waiting for a DPS appointment. Bring your valid Texas restricted license, the driver education certificate for the course you completed, and an ITAD certificate dated within 90 days.

Requirements are set by Texas DPS and TDLR and are summarised here for convenience. Confirm the current checklist on dps.texas.gov before your appointment.

Road test preparation

## Road Test Preparation: What to Expect

Passing the Road Test requires more than memorizing a route. You need to demonstrate consistent vehicle control, observation, and safe decision-making.

Many adult applicants also complete the Impact Texas Adult Drivers program and the required driver education certificate before scheduling their exam.

A practice lesson cannot guarantee a pass. It can help you understand your current skill level and identify areas where you need further improvement.

- Complete stops

- Right and left turns

- Proper signaling

- Speed control

- Lane position

- Mirror and blind-spot checks

- Parking

- Backing

- Following directions

- Awareness of surrounding traffic

## Frequently Asked Questions About Adult Driving Lessons

### Can an adult take driving lessons with no previous experience?

Yes. Our adult beginner driving lessons start with the vehicle, controls, and basic actions needed to move safely — adjusting the seat and mirrors, understanding the controls, and steering at low speeds — before progressing to residential roads, intersections, and busier traffic.

### Can I take driving lessons if I already have a license?

Yes. Driving lessons for adults with a license are built for drivers who want a refresher or need practice with situations they tend to avoid, such as highways, lane changes, or parking.

### How many driving lessons do adults need before the Road Test?

It depends on your current skill level and goals. We offer 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10-hour packages so you can choose the amount of instruction that matches how much practice you still need before testing.

### How much are driving lessons for adults?

Pricing depends on the package you choose — 2, 4, 6, 8, or 10-hour driver training, or the 6-hour online permit class. Call (469) 709-7613 for current pricing on each option.

### Are private driving lessons available for nervous adults?

Yes. Our private driving lessons for adults give you one-on-one time with a licensed instructor who adapts to your comfort level, and our driving lessons for nervous adults use gradual exposure and structured practice.

### What should I bring to an adult driving lesson?

Arrive ready to drive in comfortable clothing and closed-toe shoes. If you are working toward a Texas Driver License, bring your current permit or license and any documentation your instructor has asked you to bring.

### Does the six-hour online permit class include driving lessons?

No. The Adult 6-Hour Online Permit Class covers the required education component only. Behind-the-wheel driving lessons are separate and can be booked in addition to the online class.
