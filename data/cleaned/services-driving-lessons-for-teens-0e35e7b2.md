## Teen Driving FAQs

### How much are driving lessons for teens?

Teen pricing depends on the program. Behind-the-Wheel Only training is $350, and the complete Teen 24-Hour Classroom & Driving package is $399. Online-only driver education is priced separately — call (469) 709-7613 for current pricing and what each package includes.

### How much is the teen driving lesson for 6 hours?

Teen behind-the-wheel packages follow Texas’s required 7 hours of driving plus 7 hours of observation rather than a standalone 6-hour block. If you need a specific number of in-car hours, call us and we’ll match you to the right package.

### Can a teen take driving lessons with a Learner Permit?

Yes. A valid Texas Learner License is required before behind-the-wheel training begins. Once your teen holds a permit and meets enrollment requirements, they can start in-car lessons with a licensed instructor.

### What is required for a Texas Learner Permit?

Texas DPS sets Learner License eligibility and documentation requirements, which typically include proof of identity, driver education enrollment or completion, and passing the knowledge test. Always review the current Texas DPS checklist before your appointment.

### Can my teen take behind-the-wheel lessons after completing online driver education somewhere else?

Yes. Our Teen Behind-the-Wheel Only option is built for students who have completed — or are completing — an accepted driver education course elsewhere and now need professional in-car instruction. Bring your driver education records so we can confirm eligibility.
