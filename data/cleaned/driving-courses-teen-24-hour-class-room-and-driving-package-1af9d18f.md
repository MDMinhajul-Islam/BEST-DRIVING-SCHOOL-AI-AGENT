## About this program

### Package details: 24 Hr Classroom + Driving Package

The 24-Hour Classroom, 7-Hour Driving, and 7-Hour Observation Course in Texas is designed to provide potential drivers with the necessary knowledge and skills required to safely operate a motor vehicle.

### 24 Hours of Classroom Instruction:

The classroom portion covers essential driving concepts, including:

- Texas traffic laws and regulations

- Road signs, signals, and pavement markings

- Defensive driving techniques

- Safe vehicle operation

- Driver responsibility and decision-making

- Hazard recognition and accident prevention

- Highway and city driving strategies

- Weather and road condition awareness

- Construction zones and work-area safety

- Risk management and safe driving habits

### 7 Hours of Behind-the-Wheel Driving Instruction

Students receive hands-on driving experience with a certified instructor while practicing essential driving skills in real-world traffic situations. This training helps students develop confidence, improve vehicle control, and learn safe driving habits.

### 7 Hours of In-Car Observation

The observation portion allows students to ride in the vehicle and observe other student drivers during their driving lessons. While not actively driving, students gain valuable insight by watching how different traffic situations are handled and how instructors provide guidance and feedback.
