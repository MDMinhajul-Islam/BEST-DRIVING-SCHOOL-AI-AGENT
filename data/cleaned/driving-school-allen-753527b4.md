## Texas Driver License Process for Allen Residents

The exact licensing path depends on the applicant’s age, previous license history, and selected driver education method. Requirements can change; confirm current rules with the Texas Department of Public Safety and TDLR.

01

### Choose the Correct Driver Education Path

Teen applicants may use a licensed driving school, an approved Parent-Taught Driver Education program, or another eligible method.

First-time adult applicants ages 18 through 24 generally need a six-hour adult driver education course. Adults age 25 or older are generally not required to complete that course.

02

### Obtain the Appropriate Learner License

Teen drivers can generally apply for a learner license beginning at age 15 after meeting the applicable education, enrollment, identification, and testing requirements.

Adult beginners need the appropriate restricted license before practicing on public roads.

03

### Complete Behind-the-Wheel Training

Teen students complete the required instructor-led driving, observation, and supervised-practice components.

Adult students may schedule private lessons according to their experience and preparation needs.

Adult Driving Lessons

04

### Maintain the Required Records

Teen and parent-taught students should retain their DE-964, driving log, school-enrollment documents, and other program records.

Adults ages 18 through 24 should retain the applicable ADE-1317 or accepted driver education certificate.

05

### Complete Impact Texas Drivers

Teen applicants generally complete Impact Texas Teen Drivers, or ITTD. Adult applicants complete the applicable Impact Texas Adult Drivers program, or ITAD.

The certificate must remain current on the driving-test date.

06

### Pass the Driving Skills Test

Eligible applicants may test through DPS or a DPS-authorized Third-Party Skills Testing provider.

Passing the driving test satisfies the skills-test requirement. DPS remains responsible for issuing the Texas driver license.
