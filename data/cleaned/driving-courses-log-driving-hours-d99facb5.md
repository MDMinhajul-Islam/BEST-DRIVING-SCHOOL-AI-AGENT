### Package details: 10 hours

Texas requires teen drivers to complete 30 hours of logged driving with an approved adult before taking the road test. Preparation is essential for any test, and the road test is no exception. The instructors at Best Driving School can help teens prepare for the upcoming exam, teaching them defensive driving techniques, best practices for safe operation of a vehicle, and providing practice time to ensure adequate knowledge of traffic laws and regulations. Additionally, they can discuss good route selection strategies and share general tips on how to successfully pass the road test. It is essential that teens take this preparation seriously in order to achieve a positive outcome.
