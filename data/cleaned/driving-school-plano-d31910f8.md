## Texas Licensing Process for Plano Residents

The exact licensing path depends on the applicant’s age, previous license history, and type of driver education. The following provides a general sequence. Requirements can change; confirm current rules with the Texas Department of Public Safety and TDLR.

01

### Select the Correct Driver Education Method

Teen applicants may use a licensed driving school, an eligible Parent-Taught Driver Education program, or another approved method.

Adults ages 18 through 24 applying for a first Texas license generally need the six-hour adult course.

02

### Obtain the Appropriate Learner License

Teen students may apply for a learner license after reaching the required age and completing the applicable initial classroom requirement.

Adult beginners need the appropriate Texas learner or restricted license before practicing on public roads.

03

### Complete Education and Behind-the-Wheel Training

Teen students complete the required classroom, instructor-led driving, observation, and supervised-practice components.

Adults can schedule private driving lessons according to their current skill level.

Adult Driving Lessons

04

### Receive the Applicable Course Certificate

Qualifying teen driver education programs issue a DE-964 certificate. Qualifying six-hour adult driver education programs issue an ADE-1317 certificate.

Keep the applicable document available for the DPS or third-party testing process.

05

### Complete Impact Texas Drivers

Teen applicants generally complete Impact Texas Teen Drivers, or ITTD. Adult applicants complete the Impact Texas program required for their age and driver education path.

The certificate must remain valid on the test date.

06

### Pass the Driving Skills Test

Eligible applicants may complete the test through DPS or an authorized Third-Party Skills Testing provider.

Passing the test is not the same as receiving the physical license; applicants must complete the remaining DPS issuance process.

## Driving School in Plano — FAQs

### Is Best Driving School licensed by TDLR?

Yes. Best Driving School LLC is listed as a current Texas driver education provider under license C3475. TDLR regulates driver education providers, while DPS handles driver licenses and third-party testing authorization.

### What driving programs are available in Plano?

### Do I need a learner permit before taking driving lessons in Plano?

Yes. Teens and adults must hold the appropriate valid Texas learner or restricted license before operating a vehicle during behind-the-wheel instruction on public roads.

### How many driving hours do Texas teens need?

Teen driver education includes seven hours of instructor-led driving and seven hours of in-car observation, plus 30 additional supervised hours with at least 10 completed at night.

### Do adults need a six-hour driver education course in Texas?

First-time applicants ages 18 through 24 generally must complete a six-hour adult driver education course. Adults age 25 or older are generally not required to take it but may still benefit from professional lessons.

### Can international drivers take lessons at Best Driving School?

Yes. Lessons can focus on Texas right-of-way rules, complete stops, lane selection, speed management, parking, and examiner expectations.

### Can I take my road test at Best Driving School in Plano?

Eligible teen and adult applicants can schedule an official Class C driving skills test at our DPS-authorized Plano location. Confirm permit, course, Impact Texas, and vehicle requirements before booking.

### What should I bring to a third-party road test?

Commonly: a valid Texas learner or restricted license, your DE-964 or ADE-1317 certificate, the applicable Impact Texas certificate, teen supervised-driving documentation, Verification of Enrollment when applicable, identification, and vehicle and insurance documents if using a personal vehicle. Review the current checklist before scheduling.

### Is a third-party road test the same as a DPS driving test?

It is the same official Class C driving skills test, administered by a DPS-authorized school. It is not an easier version. DPS remains responsible for issuing the license.

### Does Best Driving School offer weekend lessons in Plano?

Yes, weekend availability is advertised. Exact openings depend on instructor and testing schedules, so reserve preferred times early.

### Can Best Driving School guarantee that I will pass on the first attempt?

No responsible driving school can guarantee a passing result. Lessons and preparation improve readiness, but each applicant must demonstrate the required skills independently during the official test.
