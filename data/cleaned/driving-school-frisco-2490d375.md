## Texas Driver License Process for Frisco Residents

The correct licensing process depends on the applicant’s age, previous licensing history, and education method. Requirements can change; confirm current rules with the Texas Department of Public Safety and TDLR.

01

### Choose the Correct Driver Education Path

Teen applicants may complete an instructor-led program, an eligible online course with in-car training, or an approved Parent-Taught Driver Education course.

First-time applicants ages 18 through 24 generally complete a six-hour adult driver education course. Adults age 25 or older are generally not required to complete driver education.

02

### Obtain the Appropriate Learner License

An eligible teen may generally apply for a learner license beginning at age 15 after completing the applicable classroom, knowledge-test, identification, residency, and school-enrollment requirements.

Adult beginners need the appropriate Texas restricted license before practicing on public roads.

03

### Complete Behind-the-Wheel Training

Teen driver education includes seven instructor-led driving hours and seven observation hours. Teens also complete 30 additional supervised driving hours, including at least 10 hours at night.

Adult students select professional practice according to their experience and training needs.

Adult Driving Lessons

04

### Keep the Required Certificates and Records

Teen and parent-taught students should retain the applicable DE-964, driving log, transfer records, and school documents.

Adult applicants ages 18 through 24 should retain the applicable ADE-1317 or other accepted driver education certificate.

05

### Complete Impact Texas Drivers

Teen applicants generally complete Impact Texas Teen Drivers. Adult applicants complete the Impact Texas program appropriate to their age and education path.

The certificate must remain valid on the driving-test date.

06

### Pass the Driving Skills Test

Eligible applicants may test through DPS or a DPS-authorized Third-Party Skills Testing provider.

Passing the skills test does not automatically issue the physical driver license. DPS remains responsible for completing the application and issuing the license.
