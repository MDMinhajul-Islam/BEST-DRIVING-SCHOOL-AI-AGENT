## What Makes a Good Driving School in Texas — And What to Look For

Four things every family should check before enrolling anywhere — including here.

01

### What Makes a Good Driving School in Texas?

#### Three Clear Standards

A good driving school in Texas meets three clear standards: it holds active TDLR certification, its instructors are individually licensed (not just the school itself), and it teaches students to the actual DPS road test criteria — not just to pass a test, but to drive safely once the license is in their wallet.

The TDLR (Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation) is the state agency that licenses and oversees all Texas driving schools. A school without TDLR certification cannot legally provide driver education in Texas or issue the DE-964 certificate that teen drivers need to apply for a license. Always confirm this before enrolling.

Active TDLR certification Individually licensed instructors Actual DPS road test criteria

02

### How Driving Lessons Work

#### A Structured Sequence

The first session starts with a brief skills check — even for complete beginners. The instructor sets baseline expectations, adjusts mirrors and seating, and starts in a low-traffic area. Lessons then progress through a structured sequence: vehicle control, residential streets, active city driving, multi-lane roads, highway entry and exit, and eventually road test simulations including parallel parking, three-point turns, and intersection navigation.

At Best Driving School, every lesson is in a dual-control vehicle. The instructor has an independent brake at all times. This isn’t just for safety — it’s what allows a complete beginner to learn without the anxiety that comes from feeling like a mistake could cause an accident. Students learn faster when the stakes feel manageable.

1. Vehicle control 2. Residential streets 3. City driving & multi-lane roads 4. Highway entry and exit 5. Parallel parking, three-point turns & intersections

03

### What Beginners Should Expect

#### Confidence Builds Quickly

Most first-time drivers are surprised by how quickly their confidence builds in the first two sessions. The instructor guides every action out loud — you’re not expected to figure anything out on your own. The biggest barrier for most beginners isn’t ability. It’s not knowing what to focus on. A good instructor narrows that down immediately.

For teens specifically: the road test is not as intimidating as it seems. Texas DPS evaluators score four things — control, observation, positioning, and signaling. Every lesson at Best Driving School is structured around building those four competencies deliberately, not just accumulating seat time.
