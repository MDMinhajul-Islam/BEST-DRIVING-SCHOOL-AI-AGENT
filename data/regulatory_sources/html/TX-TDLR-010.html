Am I Eligible to be a Parent-Taught Instructor?

Parent-taught driver education in Texas allows a parent, stepparent, grandparent, step-grandparent, foster parent or legal guardian to teach their teenager how to drive. Parents and legal guardians may also designate an alternate instructor who must meet the same eligibility requirements. Parent taught instructors may not charge a fee for conducting the course.
