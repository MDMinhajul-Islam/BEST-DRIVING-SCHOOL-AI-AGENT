Am I Eligible to be a Parent-Taught Instructor?
Parent-taught driver education in Texas allows a parent, stepparent, grandparent, step-grandparent, foster parent or legal guardian to teach their teenager how to drive. Parents and legal guardians may also designate an alternate instructor who must meet the same eligibility requirements.
Parent taught instructors may not charge a fee for conducting the course.
Answer the questions below to determine if you’re eligible to be an instructor.
1. What is your relationship to the student?
2. Are you at least 25 years of age?
3. Have you held a driver’s license for at least seven years?
4. Have you continuously held a valid driver's license for the past three years?
5. Have you ever been convicted of criminally negligent homicide?
6. Have you been convicted of DWI within the past seven years?
7. Has your driver's license been suspended, revoked or forfeited because of traffic violations during the past three years?
8. Have you been involved in two or more accidents have you that were deemed to be your fault in the past three years?
9. Have you had three or more moving violations in the past three years?
