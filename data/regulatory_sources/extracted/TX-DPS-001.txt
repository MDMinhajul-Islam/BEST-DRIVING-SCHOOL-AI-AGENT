Default Text Only L15: # Apply for a Texas Driver License

November 25, 2024

Before your driver license appointment, use the REAL ID Document Check tool.  This quick online tool asks you questions and creates a personalized list of exactly what documents you will need.

Use resources like the Texas Driver’s Handbook and "What it takes to Apply for a DL or ID Card" brochure for more information.

If you already have a Texas ID, you will need to surrender it when you get you apply for a driver license.
Step 1.  Gather the Required Documentation:
1. Proof of U.S. Citizenship or Lawful Presence (if not a U.S. Citizen)
2. Proof of Texas Residency L27:   3. Proof of Identity L28:   4. Social Security Number
5. Evidence of current Texas Vehicle Registration for each vehicle you own. Visit Texas Vehicle Registration†renew.txdmv.gov website for more information.  Proof of vehicle registration applies to new residents who are surrendering an out-of-state driver license only.
6. Proof of Insurance for each vehicle you own or a statement affirming you do not own a vehicle.
7. Impact Texas Driver (ITD) certificate, if you will be taking a driving test for the issuance of your driver license.  The certificate must be dated within 90 days for your driving skills test.
8. A six-hour adult Driver Education†www.tdlr.texas.gov course, if you are 18 through 24 years of age and applying for your first Texas driver license.  This requirement is waived for new residents 18 or older who are surrendering a valid, unexpired driver license from another state.  (No driver education requirements for ages 25 and older)
Step 2.  Visit a Driver License Office:
1. Visit Driver License Services – Appointments to schedule an appointment at your local driver license office.
2. Complete the application , available online or at the office.
3. Provide the following documentation to the license and permit specialist:
1. Completed driver license Application form
2. Proof of U.S. Citizenship or Lawful Presence (if not a U.S. Citizen)
3. Proof of Texas Residency , and
4. Proof of Identity .
4. Provide Biometric Information (signature and thumbprints)
5. Have your picture taken.
6. Pay the application fee .
7. Pass the vision exam.
8. Pass the written knowledge and a practical driving skills test to demonstrate your understanding of traffic laws and safe driving practices. You may complete the testing requirements at the DPS office or with a Third Party provider .
Step 3.  The Final Steps:

Once your driver license has been issued, you will receive a temporary driver license that allows you to drive legally while you wait for your permanent card.
* Before leaving the driver license office, carefully review the printed information on your temporary driver license for accuracy.  If any of the information is incorrect, please notify the license and permit specialist.
* Your official driver license will typically arrive by mail within 2-3 weeks.  To check the mailing status of your card, visit our mailing status web page.
